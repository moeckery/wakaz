The bell and QR are the same official Linear artwork shown in the approved preview. The check uses the author's single-check `Unread.svg`, not the double-check `Check Read.svg`. All original path geometry and 1.5px strokes are preserved. Black fill and stroke attributes are normalized to `currentColor`; QR fill modules remain filled. No duotone layers are used.

The sprite contains only the three pinned SVG sources listed above. The deployed app needs no icon package or external request. Keep the visible About attribution and the accompanying SOLAR-LICENSE.txt notice.
